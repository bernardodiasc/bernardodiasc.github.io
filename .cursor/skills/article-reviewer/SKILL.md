description: Analyzes articles for clarity, tone, structure, and provides constructive feedback.
---

# Instructions

When asked to review an article, follow these steps:

1.  **Analyze Structure:** Check if the article has a clear introduction, body paragraphs, and conclusion.
2.  **Evaluate Content:** Identify the main argument (thesis) and check if supporting evidence is provided.
3.  **Critique Tone:** Assess if the tone is appropriate for the target audience.
4.  **Identify Improvements:** Suggest specific changes for clarity, brevity, or tone.
5.  **Summarize:** Provide a brief summary and a "score" out of 10.

*   @file: Refer to the article file being reviewed.
